# Presentation route

1. Begin Mission → Start EVA. Walk to Habitat diagnostics and run diagnostics.
2. Visit each of the three solar collectors. Clean each and wait for “Collector online”.
3. Walk to Rover service bay. Open the service bay and recover the battery.
4. Walk to Power junction. Install the battery, turn connectors to 1 / 2 / 3,
   then engage the relay. The habitat lights come online.
5. Return to Habitat diagnostics. Allocate reserves and prioritize life support.
   The dust front arrives: light fades, wind rises, and visibility drops.
6. Walk to Meridian airlock and enter shelter. Seal the airlock at the shelter
   console, then transmit status. The report shows the fully prepared ending.
7. Replay to demonstrate a different choice. Shelter early for a less prepared
   survival result, or stay outside through the front for emergency evacuation.

Talk to the crew along the way to show dialogue choices and morale feedback.
Inspect water shielding inside the habitat. Use the journal to review missed
dialogue, and Pause / audio to demonstrate mute and reduced motion.

For a short presentation, navigation buttons handle walking around obstacles;
the player still performs all repairs. Use WASD and drag to show free exploration.
Headphones help demonstrate positional machinery and the storm audio transition.
The preparation clock pauses in menus, so explanation time does not cost reserves.

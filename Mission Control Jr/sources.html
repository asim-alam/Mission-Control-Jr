MISSION CONTROL JR. / LEARNING REFERENCES

Sources & credits

The game is fictional and simplified. These sources support its learning ideas; they do not validate its simulation values.

NASA and partner-agency references

  • NASA Space Apps 2026: Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer
  • NASA/JPL: Mars rovers braving severe dust storms
  • NASA Science: Solar-panel dust accumulation and cleanings
  • NASA: MOXIE oxygen demonstration
  • NASA: Radiation protection concepts for Mars
  • NASA: Mars communications disruption and delay
  • NASA/JPL Robotics: rover terrain assessment and navigation
  • NASA Science: How Perseverance drives on Mars
  • NASA/JPL: Curiosity’s May 2024 solar storm. Base Boss scales the reported 8,100 microgray observation to training points and uses fictional shelter factors.
  • NASA CCMC DONKI: historical space weather — the shelter hazard timing and exposure points are authored.
  • NASA OSD-269: ISS lettuce study — the 16:8 light schedule is a reference; Mars growth and yield are fictional.
  • Khodadad et al. (2020), Microbiological and Nutritional Analysis of Lettuce Crops Grown on the ISS — reports the VEG-03A fan status anomaly and explains limits on causal interpretation of environmental and microbial results.
  • NASA: Growing Plants in Space — Veggie growing system and plant pillows.
  • NASA: Plant Water Management — water delivery in microgravity.
  • NASA JPL Horizons: planetary light-time — center-to-center values approximate the fictional surface missions.
  • NASA LOLA lunar polar illumination — long-term averages provide context, not a short-term shadow forecast.
  • NASA Blue Ghost: electrodynamic dust shield demonstration — the game’s rigid-panel rack is fictional.
  • NASA Glenn: lunar surface propagation — the ridge link is a simplified teaching model.

Archived records, checksums and model limitations are available in data provenance and model assumptions.

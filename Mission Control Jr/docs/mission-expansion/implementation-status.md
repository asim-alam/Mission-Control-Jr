# Expansion implementation status

Working record, 8 November 2026. The six new missions are playable and their cards are visible; release acceptance is not complete. A working prototype or a passing unit suite alone does not establish that every requirement in the expansion plan has been met.

## Implemented

- Added Mars missions Solar Shelter, The Greenhouse Promise and Waiting for Earth; added separate Moon missions Chasing the Sun, Dust at the Door and Beyond the Ridge. Each has a controller, 3D scene, guided interaction, resource consequences, debrief and replay snapshot. Controller tests cover alternative strategies and interlocks.
- Added independent Mars and Moon campaign selection and progress, version 1 migration, registry-based routing, practice sampler, and base-path-safe query links. The Thirty Sols exercise remains separate. New surface missions save versioned controller snapshots on this device and offer a clear continue/restart choice after reload; persistence failures leave play available and explain that this browser cannot save.
- Added original procedural generators and playable GLBs for new survival and lunar props, with named nodes, manifest metadata, GLB validation and loader tests. A central `mission-assets.js` registry maps each new chapter to build-tracked GLBs. Deliberate double-clicking on nearby 3D station props dispatches the same station action as E and the labeled button; the pointer action respects player reach and is disposed with its scene. Source and build commands are in the README and asset references.
- Archived and normalized NASA DONKI, OSDR, JPL Horizons, lunar LOLA illumination samples and NASA EDS material with checksums and provenance. The modeled gameplay values and the limits of each data connection are stated in `public/data/model-assumptions.json` and the mission copy.
- Repaired or improved the existing storm, rover and Thirty Sols systems, including explicit battery state, time-based allocation, conserved shielded water, distinct water/rover shelter outcomes, nonzero modeled exposure, nonlethal crew-condition limits, future crop supplements, seeded events, packet state, and separately recorded attempt completion, earned success, and mastery. The F01–F12 implementation/test mapping follows below; cross-mission browser and human validation remain open.
- Added a lower-effects setting for the six new missions, applied on mission start; it reduces render pixel density, antialiasing and lunar shadows. Standard and low modes were sampled on the Intel UHD 620. Simple mode now shows a short mission-specific decision tip and limits long menus to two choices plus “More choices”; Details mode adds source-specific limits and exposes all choices.
- Fixed two cross-mission browser bugs found during acceptance: station action buttons now dispatch their stable station ID, and an active saved attempt opens a Continue/Restart choice after reload instead of replaying an opening action against the active state. Added regression coverage for the station-button event binding and Simple/Details option presentation.
- Added a practice sampler with real entry snapshots; practice completion does not award campaign completion. The greenhouse sampler was checked in the browser.

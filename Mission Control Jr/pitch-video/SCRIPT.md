# MISSION CONTROL JR. — "BEFORE THE STORM"
### A 4-minute cinematic pitch film · BONGO APHELION · NASA Space Apps 2026

**Runtime:** 3:55 (5 s under the 240 s limit) · **Aspect:** 2.39:1 letterbox throughout (gameplay framed inside the bars) · **VO:** ~430 words, one calm, warm narrator, never rushed.

**Look**
- Mars scenes: warm amber and rust grade.
- Space and Earth: cold teal.
- Title cards: thin white serif text on black, slow 1.5 s fades.
- No whooshes or flashy transitions. Use hard cuts on music beats, and dissolves only when time passes.

**Sound motif:** a soft **battery tone**, a single low pulse like a heartbeat. It slows as Opportunity dies (Act 1). It returns as the player charges the outpost (Act 3). It ends strong and steady in the final shot. The same sound carries the whole story.

**Music arc**
- Act 1: an ambient drone with Mars wind.
- Act 2: a lone piano motif.
- Act 3: a pulse and percussion build.
- Act 4: a full swell, a drop to silence, then the final title hit.

**P + E + U**
- **Preparation** is the theme: preparation decides survival.
- **Emotion** comes from Opportunity's last message and the crew we keep safe.
- **Urgency:** the kids who will plan the Moon and Mars missions are in school today.

**240 Seconds of Glory map:** WHO 0:00–0:55 · WHY 0:55–1:50 · WHAT 1:50–3:05 · HOW 3:05–3:55

## Capture notes for the game footage (the cinematic part)
- Record at **1440p or 4K, 60 fps**, then grade and crop to 2.39:1. Hide the HUD for beauty and montage shots, and keep it visible for decision shots (16, 18, 19).
- Use the **third-person camera at low angles**, with slow movement only: walk slowly, turn slowly. Fast cuts come from the edit, not the camera.
- Record each mission beat 3 times and keep the best 6–9 seconds.
- Must-have shots: the dust wall approaching Meridian (6, M3), the decision dialog (16), the shelter door at dawn (23), the Base Boss golden-hour drive (20), and every GAME column shot in the montage table.

## Honesty checklist
- "My battery is low and it's getting dark" is a journalist's plain-English reading of the final data, and the script frames it that way.
- "Nine missions" means 6 on Mars and 3 on the Moon. Base Boss is the free-roam hub.
- The NASA data is real but **archived, not live**, so the script says "real", never "live". Live space weather is listed as a next step.
- There have been no kid playtests yet, so impact is stated as a **goal**, not a result.
- "More languages" is a proposed next step. Confirm the team agrees.

MERIDIAN NETWORK · APEX EXPEDITION

👨‍🚀 Welcome, Base Boss!

Commander Reyes: “Welcome to the Meridian Basin rover fleet! You have full command of the network. Our remote outposts need our help to keep life support, food, water, and radio links running smoothly.”

📋 Commander’s Network Checklist:

  • 🗺️ 1. Select an Outpost: Click any base on the map (Verdant, Horizon, Ridge Relay).
  • 🚙 2. Travel Across Mars: Drive manually with WASD or click “Drive there for me” (Autopilot) to cruise safely.
  • 🧑‍🚀 3. Step Outside (E): Park at the station, press E (or tap Step outside), and walk to the gold beacon.
  • 📦 4. Complete Jobs: Deliver water recycler valves, transport greenhouse salads, and test Earth radio relays.
  • 🏆 5. Final Mission Report: Return to Meridian Base to submit your expedition report to Elena Reyes!

🎮 Rover Controls: W/S to accelerate/reverse, A/D to steer, Space to brake, E to exit/board rover, V to change camera view.

🌟 You’re in command now, Cadet. Let’s keep Mars Base thriving!
